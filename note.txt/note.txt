DESCRIPTION:

THAMILI AI is an AI-powered Video and Multimedia Generation Studio that combines 
Tamil cultural aesthetics with futuristic cyberpunk design. The platform is 
designed for creators, directors, writers, and artists to generate, manage,
 and explore AI-powered multimedia content.

The website provides Prompt-to-Video Generation, Image-to-Video Animation,
 Character Consistency, AI Prompt Enhancement, AI Image Generation, AI Chat,
 Video History, Custom Video Playback, Tamil/English language support, 
and token/credit management.

The application follows a hybrid full-stack architecture, consisting of a
 Vite + React frontend, an Express + Node.js backend, and external AI 
services such as Google Gemini/Veo and OpenRouter.
